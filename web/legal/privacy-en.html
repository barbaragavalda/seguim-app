Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This policy explains what data the Seguim! app collects, what we use it for, and what rights you have over it.

Data controller

Bàrbara Gavaldà
Contact email: barbaragavalda@gmail.com

What data we collect

  • Account data: username, email address (stored encrypted), and password (stored as a hash, never in plain text).
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account identifier and the associated email address, used only to authenticate you.
  • Your preferred language in the app.
  • In-app activity: the series and movies you follow, the episodes you've marked as watched, your favorites, and any custom lists you create.
  • If you import data from TV Time: we process the file you upload solely to transfer that watch history into your account.

Why we collect it

Only to provide the service: keeping your account, remembering what you've watched, and helping you track series and movies. We never use your data for advertising purposes.

Who we share it with

  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google, to verify your identity.
  • TheTVDB: we use this service to fetch series/movie information (titles, artwork, synopses) — we never send them any of your personal data.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or analytics.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account stays active. You can delete your account at any time from the app (Profile → Delete account), which permanently and immediately erases all your data.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data, as well as to request a copy of it. You can exercise these rights directly from the app (editing your profile, deleting your account) or by contacting us at barbaragavalda@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Security

Your email address is stored encrypted, and your password is never stored in plain text - only as an irreversible hash.

Cookies

Our server uses a technical session cookie, required for it to function. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant updates to this policy, we'll notify you within the app.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, write to us at barbaragavalda@gmail.com.
